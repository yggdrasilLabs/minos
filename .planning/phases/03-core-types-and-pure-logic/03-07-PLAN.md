| Judged | 4 | digest(exp), digest(judge), u64(trial) |
| Selected | 5 | u64(generation) |

| Steered | 7 | u64(generation), u64(ordinal) |

| Reviewed | 10 | digest(request) |
| ReportAcknowledged | 11 | digest(exp), digest(report) |

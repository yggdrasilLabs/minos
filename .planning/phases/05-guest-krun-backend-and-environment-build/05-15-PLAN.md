## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| backend -> helper process | The supervisor spawns and kills an entitled, Seatbelt-confined process |
| helper stdout/stderr -> supervisor | Guest-influenced console and VMM log bytes |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-15-01 | Tampering | Guest text forging a watchdog marker to force infrastructure retries or a timeout | high | mitigate | classify matches markers as exact whole lines together with their exit codes; the logger prefixes every logged line (05-07); tests include forged prefixes and embedded markers |
| T-05-15-02 | Denial of service | A VM outliving its plan | high | mitigate | Group kill at max duration + 30 s; helper watchdog at max duration; Reaper drop kill; parent-death watchdog and sweep (05-11) |
| T-05-15-03 | Information disclosure | Host environment reaching the helper or guest | high | mitigate | env_clear on spawn (tested with /usr/bin/env), stdin null; exit_env (05-19) |
| T-05-15-04 | Elevation of privilege | A launch without Seatbelt | high | mitigate | Every spawn goes through seatbelt::argv; preflight refuses when the layer is missing (05-06) |
| T-05-15-05 | Denial of service | Console flooding the host | medium | mitigate | Console read through cap + 1 and the group killed (pipe) or 125 marker (file) |
| T-05-15-06 | Repudiation | Loosening the EPERM check without proof | medium | mitigate | 20-run investigation recorded; EPERM accepted only with zombie proof |
| T-05-15-07 | Information disclosure | Input disk with judge data left on disk | medium | mitigate | Job directory removed when KrunRunning drops; sweep removes orphans |

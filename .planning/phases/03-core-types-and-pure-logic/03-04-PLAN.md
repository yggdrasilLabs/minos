Exact arithmetic used by Cost (document it in money.rs): scaled = vcpu_rate_pico * vcpu_ms * 1024 + gib_second_rate_pico * mem_mib_ms + sum over token kinds of token_rate_pico * tokens * 1_024_000. The unit of `scaled` is 1e-12 USD divided by 1_024_000 (1000 ms per s times 1024 MiB per GiB). micro_dollars = (scaled + 512_000_000_000) / 1_024_000_000_000 (round half up). Known vectors: rate "0.0000276944" per vCPU-second (27_694_400 pico) for 3_600_000 vCPU-ms gives 99_700 micro-dollars and `approx. cost: $0.10`; rate "0.0000036667" per GiB-second for 1_024_000 MiB-ms (one GiB-second) gives 4 micro-dollars; rate "0.0000166667" per GiB-second for 1024 MiB over 3_600_000 ms gives 60_000 micro-dollars; rate "0.000003" per input token for 1_000_000 tokens gives 3_000_000 micro-dollars and `approx. cost: $3.00`.

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-11-01 | Elevation of privilege | A launch on a host whose Seatbelt profile stopped enforcing | high | mitigate | Offline denial probes with controls; inconclusive fails closed as SeatbeltNotEnforcing; preflight refuses without the Seatbelt layer (05-06) |

| T-05-11-05 | Tampering | PID reuse making the sweep kill an unrelated process | medium | mitigate | ps ppid and exact command match before any kill; EPERM is Unverified, never gone |
| T-05-11-06 | Tampering | A symlinked or shared job root | medium | mitigate | JobRoot refuses symlinks, other owners, and modes other than 0700 |

Write `supervise.rs` to E-4 and E-5. Key rules:
- `run` spawns `std::process::Command::new(<argv first>)` with the remaining argv, `env_clear()`, `stdin(Stdio::null())`, stdout and stderr to files created with `create_new` in the job directory, and `std::os::unix::process::CommandExt::process_group(0)`. After the child exits, accept whatever connections are already pending, once, then stop. Per-connection caps come from `conn_caps` (the last value repeats); after `max_connections`, stop accepting. A read that hits the cap marks `overflowed`, keeps exactly `cap` bytes, and drops the stream, so the guest's next write fails.

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Guest to host (vsock connections, console file, helper stdout and stderr) | Every byte is guest-influenced and hostile; it is capped on read and leaves only through validating or sanitizing methods |
| Supervisor to minos-vmm (argv, environment, fds) | The helper gets no environment, no stdin, no TTY, and its own process group |
| Spike evidence printing | Console text reaches the executor's terminal and the committed evidence |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-29 | Denial of Service | vsock output flood | high | mitigate | Per-connection `take(cap + 1)` with an overflow flag; the stream is dropped at the cap; max connection count; unit tests at and over the cap |
| T-02-30 | Denial of Service | console and stdio flood | medium | mitigate | Files read back through caps; minos-vmm's watchdog (02-03) stops the VM at the console cap |
| T-02-31 | Tampering | host environment or stdin reaching the helper | high | mitigate | `env_clear`, `Stdio::null`, file-backed stdout and stderr; unit test with /usr/bin/env |
| T-02-32 | Denial of Service | helper outliving its deadline | medium | mitigate | Host deadline with kill and wait; own process group for Phase 5's group kill; unit test with /bin/sleep |
